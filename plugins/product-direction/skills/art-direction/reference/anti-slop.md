# Anti-slop gate

AI slop is not ugliness. It is **a stack of individually reasonable defaults, none chosen for this subject.** A visitor recognises it in under two seconds. Use this file two ways: before designing (list the defaults the brief attracts and pre-refuse them) and when reviewing (count tells; three or more means redesign the language, not the pixels).

## The tells, and what replaces them

| # | Tell | Why it reads as generated | Replace with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purple→blue or indigo→pink gradient hero, glow orbs | The single most generated background since 2023 | A flat, chosen ground colour; atmosphere only from real imagery or a purposeful scrim |
| 2 | Inter/Poppins/Geist Sans alone, 600 weight, default tracking | No typographic decision was made | Characterful display + systematic workhorse; extreme weights; tracking that tightens with size |
| 3 | Centered hero: pill badge → H1 with one gradient word → grey sub → two buttons → screenshot | The template answer | Hero built from the premise: type-as-image, the real artifact, a world, or one statement |
| 4 | Three equal feature cards with emoji or line icons | Content fitted to a layout instead of the reverse | Index rows, a numbered process, a roster, a spec table — whatever the content's real shape is |
| 5 | Glassmorphism cards, soft shadows, 16–24px radius on everything | Depthless "premium" costume | Hairline rules, flat planes, one radius decision (0, 2–4px, or full pill) used consistently |
| 6 | "Revolutionize / Supercharge / Unlock / Seamless / Next-gen / Elevate" | Words that fit any product | Concrete verbs and nouns from the subject; the product's actual claim |
| 7 | Round fake stats ("10k+ users", "99% satisfaction"), fake logo walls, stock testimonials | Fabricated credibility — the one thing visitors actively catch | Verified proof next to its claim, or an honest placeholder flagged to the user |
| 8 | Every section fades up 40px over 600ms+ | Motion applied uniformly, so none of it means anything | One signature moment; line-mask reveals on headlines only; everything else simply present |
| 9 | Floating 3D blobs, abstract spheres, generic AI renders | Imagery that illustrates nothing | Real product, real work, authored 3D that explains, or no imagery |
| 10 | Bento grid hero for a non-dashboard product | 2026's default SaaS layout | A layout derived from the content's shape |
| 11 | Pure `#000`/`#fff`, slate/zinc greys everywhere | Framework defaults | Warm near-black and off-white; neutrals tinted toward the ground |
| 12 | Dark mode + neon accent "because tech" | Category cliché | Colour chosen from the premise; dark only if the premise is dark |
| 13 | Custom cursor blob with `mix-blend-mode: difference` on a site with no reason for it | 2021 Awwwards cliché copied forward | Native cursor, or a cursor that carries a label with meaning |
| 14 | Marquee logo strip of invented or unlicensed logos | Fake proof in motion | Real logos with permission, or nothing |
| 15 | Section headings like "Features", "Why choose us", "How it works", "Testimonials" | Template scaffolding exposed | Headings that say the thing ("Live in weeks, not quarters"), with an index label if structure is needed |
| 16 | Identical padding and density in every section | No rhythm | Alternate airy statement sections with dense information sections |
| 17 | Preloader percentage counter with no meaning | Delay dressed as craft | No preloader, or a curtain that carries the premise, ≤ 1.5s, skipped on repeat visits |
| 18 | Footer: four link columns + newsletter + socials, tiny | Afterthought | Footer as finale: closing statement or oversized wordmark, primary contact action |

## Second-order slop: the 2025–26 "premium template"

Once models learned to avoid tell #1, they converged on a new set of looks that `frontend-design` also names: **warm cream ground + high-contrast serif display + terracotta accent**; **near-black ground + one acid-green or vermilion accent**; and **broadsheet** hairline-rule, zero-radius newspaper columns. Several 2026 award sites use relatives of these *with a premise* (Boon's bone + signal orange, Oxigen's hairlines). The test is not resemblance — it is whether the choice traces to the subject. If the same palette and grid would have come out for a different brief in the same category, it is slop in better clothes.

## Review scoring

- **0–2 tells:** polish-level issues; fix in place.
- **3–5 tells:** the design language is underspecified; revisit `### Refusals` and the premise.
- **6+ tells:** start from the premise again. Restyling will produce a better-dressed template.

## Traps when "fixing" slop

- Swapping one trend for another (gradients → grain + bento + mono) without a premise. It is still a collage.
- Maximalism as an escape. Loud is not distinctive; coherent is.
- Copying a reference site's palette and face. That is someone else's language, and visitors in the same category recognise it.
- Removing all motion. Stillness is fine; but one directed moment is what separates premium from plain.
